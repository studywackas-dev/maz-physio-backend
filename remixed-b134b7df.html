About Us

MAZ Physio Care & Surgicals

At MAZ Physio Care & Surgicals, we provide expert physiotherapy and surgical supply services under the guidance of Dr. Umer Farook (BPT, DOFSIM, MIAP). We offer highly personalised, evidence-based treatment for a wide range of conditions.

Our clinic is equipped with modern physiotherapy equipment and follows a multidisciplinary approach to ensure the fastest and safest recovery for every patient. Home visit services are also available across Ramanathapuram.

  • Qualified BPT physiotherapist — Dr. Umer Farook
  • Member of Indian Association of Physiotherapists (MIAP)
  • Diploma in Orthopaedic & Sports (DOFSIM)
  • Home visit physiotherapy available
  • Surgical & rehabilitation equipment supply
  • Paediatric, neurological & orthopaedic care
